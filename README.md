<h2>What is Fade; Soul Apocalypse?</h2>
<p>

Fade; Soul Apocalypse is a fictional story I wrote. It tells the story of a conflict between angels and demons, involving civilization and the fate of humanity. This story itself has two main characters, namely Marshall D. Roman, and Natsugo Yuga.

  <h1></h1>
<h1>History</h1>
  <p>Long ago, there were two opposing spirit races: angels and demons. They waged constant war for power and their respective ideologies. The angels wanted to exterminate the demons because they considered them pests and the source of all evil and darkness in the world. Meanwhile, the demons wanted to exterminate the angels because they wanted freedom. Freedom from the underworld, even though it would create chaos due to their very existence... As a result of this endless war, human civilization continually collapsed. When they began to rebuild their civilization, it always collapsed due to the intense battle between light and darkness. Although this war took place in the spirit dimension, it had a significant impact on the real world. As a result, human civilization did not progress; they had to continually rebuild from scratch. Unfortunately, the impact of the battle between demons and angels caused disasters in the real world, such as massive earthquakes, devastating lightning storms, and unexplained deaths. And for a time, the progress of human civilization was halted. For 4 MILLION YEARS.</p>

<p> 2,200 years ago, there was a new angel king, Gatos Maruka. He was still the same as the previous kings. He was fiercely anti-demons. But what was different was that Gatos was even more cruel and personal than his ancestors. Not only that, he possessed immense power, surpassing all the angels in existence. And one day, there was a trade exchange between demons and angels. The chairman was Gatos's friend, Yura. Initially, Gatos suspected it was a trap, but Yura remained optimistic. She believed that starting with something small like this trade could be the starting point for peace between angels and demons. But sadly... Gatos was right. It was a trap, and the trade transaction was actually intended to kill Gatos. But in reality? Yura became the victim. At that moment, Gatos was so furious, so empty, his hatred for demons, which had already been so great, now even more so. And for the first time in history... There were angels crying and grieving. A figure that should have no emotions, now it all started with Gatos. Gatos then approached hell, where the demons resided. And without further ado, he slaughtered all the demons in hell, all the demons dying and vanishing before Gatos. Gatos swept through all the dimensional layers of hell. Until he met the king of hell, but he easily killed him. And before the king of hell perished, he said... "Who are you really? Are you really the king of angels?" Those words left Gatos speechless. He realized he had surpassed the limits of an angel. In fact... perhaps he was even more cruel than a demon at that moment. He killed so many demons just to avenge the death of one person. Destroyed... Gatos then created a vortex of light that expanded throughout all layers of hell, annihilating all existence there. Gatos stood in the silence of hell, half the demon population wiped out, and he began to reflect on what he had done...</p>

<p>Ever since that tragedy, Gatos has still been haunted by his over-the-line actions. He is deeply traumatized.
But then, he is invited to meet a new demon king: Azathoth Darkway.
Initially, Azathoth's presence was barred by the royal guards, but Azathoth broke straight through their absolute barrier. Normally, any demon that even slightly touches that barrier would have their existence corrupted and erased, but Azathoth? How could he calmly breach all 7 barriers?!
After being surrounded, Gatos finally ordered all the royal guards to fall back. Because after all, he knew this demon could easily defeat them all.
Gatos also immediately noticed something unusual that demons don't possess within Azathoth.
His existence... it wasn't that of a demon. Yet it indeed resembled one. And Gatos realized that Azathoth's purpose in coming here was not for anything evil. From this point on, Gatos begins to learn to understand demons.
In short... Azathoth has a crazy plan that many spirits had never even thought of.
He wants to seal the angels and demons away in their respective origins (the heavenly realm and the underworld). Because with them sealed away there, there would no longer be endless conflicts like this war. Why does Azathoth want that? The reason is simple yet profound.
He wants to see a more advanced, more modern human civilization. Because he once read a book depicting the life of human civilization in the future. That is why the only way to make them thrive is to put an end to the war between demons and angels.
Gatos himself was shocked by Azathoth's ideology. Even the king of angels had never thought about human life.
Eventually, they planned a ritual to send both races back to their true homelands. Because this realm had already seen far too much bloodshed, death, and warfare.
However, the political climate between the demons and angels at that time was becoming increasingly unhealthy. The demons wanted revenge by assassinating Gatos, while the angels intended to protect their king. And yes, another war was about to break out.
Then, when the war began, through a magnificent ritual, they stood together across a different dimensional layer. Using the combined powers of Gatos and Azathoth, the demons and angels were then transferred to the heavenly realm and the underworld.
The war was successfully averted, and humans were finally able to begin rebuilding their civilization free from catastrophic disasters.
At that moment, Azathoth split his essence into two parts, which would go on to live their own lives for humanity in the future.
Azathoth wanted to witness firsthand the life of human civilization in the future. And finally... they parted ways. Just like Azathoth, Gatos also reincarnated into the future, witnessing things humanity had never built before. There were so many buildings, all kinds of people, various languages, and so on...
However, Gatos's role was strictly that of an observer, as he could not stay in the human world for too long. If he lingered too long, their dimension wouldn't be able to handle Gatos's presence, causing it to eventually shatter.</p> 

  <p>In 1789, a nobleman named William visited the estate of the Altana family, a household that had been massacred by unknown creature. The place was already abandoned, and all the corpses had turned to skeletons. It seemed the incident had been left undiscovered for five years and was only just being brought to light. Following this, news of the Altana family's destruction quickly spread to other nations.
Afterwards, the heads of five prominent noble families from around the world held a meeting to discuss this terrifying event. Among them were William Nicholas, Albert Bluespark, Chalia Harmonia, Charles Ven Roslia, and Yan Xie Hō.
Charles was the one who proposed the meeting, and without wasting any time, he directly stated that the perpetrator behind the Altana family tragedy was a demon. At first, the others were skeptical of Charles's claim, but given the extraordinarily unusual scale of destruction, they could not deny that a demon was the culprit.
Beyond that, however, Charles proposed harnessing the spiritual power of demons. After his family (the Roslia family) observed a sacred flower—the Wither Rose, or black rose—they came to believe that the presence of demons in this world is not merely folklore or a biblical tale. Instead, their presence could bring about an evolution for humankind, as immortal beings endowed with spiritual power capable of shaping civilization.
Chalia briefly opposed Charles's proposal, arguing that demonic power is a force of darkness that brings destruction. However, Charles countered, claiming that humans are far more cruel than demons; their emotions and vanity push them beyond all limits. Therefore, to impose boundaries, they need to taste what it means to become the darkness itself.
Albert refuted this. He argued that while humans can indeed become more wicked than demons, they are noble creatures capable of fostering harmony for future life, and that negative human traits stem from individual attitudes.
William agreed with Albert's stance. Not all humans are evil and hypocritical, nor are all humans swallowed by darkness.
The meeting concluded. A few days later, the Roslia family continued their spiritual experiments on the Wither Rose, attempting prayers and spiritual offerings in the hope of summoning a demon into the world.
In short, some time later, while performing a ritual, a demonic figure suddenly appeared—a demon that should have been in hell had now broken out, because the seals on hell had begun to weaken due to the steady influx of offerings directed at them.
Charles finally spoke to the demon, confessing that he was the one who had summoned it to the world. At first, the demon showed no interest in Charles and intended to cause chaos, but Charles prostrated himself before it. Eventually, the demon stated its desire: it wanted to inhabit Charles's body. Consequently, it entered Charles, transforming him into a half-demon human. He could feel immense power surging within him. Yet, strangely at that moment, the demon felt that its control wasn't as absolute as it had imagined. It turned out that Charles possessed a nature far more wicked than a demon; he was truly satanic. That was why the demon could not easily control his body.
Ever since that moment, Charles truly believed that demons could bring evolution to humankind and the world. He was already disappointed and disgusted by selfish and hypocritical humans. Now, he wanted those humans to face the consequences—not through a massacre, but by forcing all of humanity to admit that they are not good people, but mere reflections of the darkness.
Charles initiated a project called the <strong>Belial Project</strong>, in which he gathered orphans and forced them to worship and perform demon-summoning rituals. However, instead of becoming vessels, the children simply died; their pure and fragile souls were not strong enough to house a demon. Yet, one child managed to survive the ritual, and he was named... Sencretz. From that moment on, Sencretz became the Roslia family's weapon of war. With Sencretz around, the other families were rendered completely helpless. Even any negotiations became useless; if they wanted to survive, they had to submit to demonic power.
Eventually, as time passed, the other noble families submitted to the Roslia family as well, forming a republic that doubled as a demon-worshipping sect.
As time continued to flow, humans began to stray further from truth, justice, and light—which ultimately gave birth to the dark entities known as wild demons.</p>

  <h1>System</h1>
  <h2>Spirit Tamer</h2>
  <p>Spirit Tamers are a group of individuals capable of more explicit control over spiritual energy. They can manifest it in various forms, including defense and attack. A Spirit Tamer must go through a special ritual called Spiritual Pressure. A ritual where one makes a contract with a chosen spirit, often an angel. And because the context is angelic, a person must have faith and a pure heart. Otherwise, the angels will not want to make a contract.

Moreover, the ritual process is very painful, where the soul of the person performing this ritual will be compressed, and will be united with the spirit of an angel. There's a chance they might fail due to incompetence, and some might even die. But if they survive and survive, they officially become Spirit Tamers.</p>
<h2>Spiritual Energy</h2>
  <p>Spiritual energy is the source of power for Spirit Tamers. This energy is formed from various materials found throughout the universe. Therefore, Spirit Tamers must be able to control and manifest their spiritual energy more controlled and carefully. Because if any Spirit Tamer fails to control their powers, the ecosystem of the universe will become uncontrollable and most likely... Collapse.</p>

  <p>There is a concept called <strong>Field</strong>. Field is a concept where a Spirit Tamer or pure spirit creates a dimension where they can manipulate the environment and its contents. However, Fields are typically identified with a type of spirit. For example, an angel's Field is associated with a bright, hopeful environment, usually symbolized by the sky. Meanwhile, the devil's Field is symbolized as a sea so deep that it feels endless, as a form of despair, destruction, and darkness.</p>

  <p>There are several levels of evolution and transformation that a Spirit Tamer can undergo:</p>
  <h3>Spirit Buff</h3>
  <p>Spirit Buff is a mode where a Spirit Tamer can increase their spiritual energy capacity, thus increasing around 10% of their endurance and 20% of the energy they can release from their original form.</p>
  <h3>Spirition</h3>
  <p>Spirition is a state where a Spirit Tamer can fully unite and acknowledge the spirit within themselves. And when that happens, a resonance effect occurs between souls, creating a significant surge of power that can also change the form of the Spirit Tamer. All their stats are increased by 100%, including endurance, speed, and attack. The energy they generate is also greater than usual. so they must remain careful not to damage the environment related to the natural ecosystem of the universe.</p>
  <h3>Spiriton Max</h3>
  <p>Spirition Max is a state where a Spirit Tamer, or pure spirit, has far surpassed the limits of a normal spirit. At this level, they are already at the level of an entity capable of influencing not just the ecosystem of the universe, but reality, even spacetime. All the status and energy they gain are much greater than the normal Spirition mode. At this point they are almost approaching the level of <strong>god complex</strong></p>
  <h3>Resurrection (Demon)</h3>
  <p>Resurrection changes can only be performed by demon Spirit Tamers or demon spirits. In this phase, they have already surpassed all the boundaries of being a spirit, life, and death, they can reach a point that only demons who can surpass the normal level of a strongest demon can reach. A point where they have completely influenced the entire existence of life and death and the boundaries of space and time that even a god cannot reach. Even their destructive power is capable of shaking the universe, <strong>or more</strong></p>
  <h3>Revelation (Angel)</h3>
  <p>Revelation is the highest transformation a Spirit Tamer, an angel, or an angelic spirit, can undergo. At this level, they receive abundant blessings, so that they can reach points where the gods cannot reach. Their existence completely affects how the universe, with its life and death, can remain in balance. To counterbalance Resurrection from demon.</p>

    <h3>Biography:</h3>
    <p>Marshall D. Roman, is a girl who was born in northern Russia, in a small village. Marshall was originally an ordinary little girl, very shy and innocent. But one day, when her little brother, Carlos, was born, she had to experience the most terrifying incident in her life.

Marshall was the human chosen to be the reincarnation vessel of Azathoth. Due to the changes and influence of Azathoth's existence, the energy Marshall released made everyone in the village turned into a skeleton. Everyone died. Only she and Carlos, the newborn, were still alive. The tragedy was the most devastating trauma in Marshall's life. As a result, he had to leave the village and take Carlos with her to survive. 

Marshall's journey to survive is very difficult, she has to take care of newborn Carlos and look for various necessities of life. And after such a long and bitter journey, Marshall met Wataru, and from that meeting, Marshall and Carlos's lives changed for the better.

Marshall learns many things from Wataru, including about spiritual energy, Spirit Tamers, and more. Marshall continues to fight and train to make herself able to protect the people she loves.

And with the support of her friends and mentors, Marshall has grown into a strong woman. After overcoming various difficult obstacles in her life, internal conflicts with her inner spirits, Marshall can finally stand up not to be protected, but to protect the people she cares about.</p>

<p>Marshall inherited much of the death essence from Azathoth, allowing her to manipulate the concepts of life and death. Even when she had become more perfect and ready to control life and death. Even though she has to go through such excruciating suffering, namely feeling the death and pain of all humans who have existed in various eras.

However, with experience, Marshall has become more accustomed to it, especially since she has a mode called <strong>Blue Reactor</strong>, a mode that allows her to completely control her life and death's reality.

Marshall is known for being a very cold girl, often showing no mercy to her enemies. Despite this, she remains caring and warm to those she loves.

Marshall's ultimate feat was when she reached the point where she had transcended his existence, at which point she had completely become the personification of the end.The end of all life, the end of all stories. Making her have very high abilities compared to others.</p>

  <h3>Biography:</h3>
  <p>Natsugo Yuga is the younger brother of Wataru Yuga and Miruka Yuga. Their parents passed away when Natsugo was just a child. Before Wataru left, he gave his hat to Natsugo in the hope that Natsugo could carry on his dream of becoming a police officer, as Wataru was leaving to continue his education in Russia. Becoming a police officer was more than just an ambition; it was Wataru's resolve to change global justice for the better, because to him, justice at that time did not truly mean justice.

After receiving news that the plane Wataru was on had crashed and his body could not be found, Natsugo felt a heavy responsibility to carry on his older brother's dream. He began training to become a police officer until he finally succeeded, alongside Miruka, who would support him.

However, while on a mission, Natsugo experienced a strange phenomenon: just as he should have been killed after being hit by a car, it was the car that got thrown back and destroyed. Natsugo then saw his own reflection in a mirror, where he was undergoing bizarre changes, such as growing horns, his eyes turning red, and his skin feeling much harder.

Natsugo later met Jezz. After Jezz explained what had happened, Natsugo was instantly shocked and confused. He was the chosen one to be a piece of Azathoth's reincarnated essence. At first, Natsugo felt he could keep all of this hidden, but after encountering and facing numerous demons—coupled with his desire to protect Miruka—Natsugo finally found the motivation to grow stronger using his demonic power.</p>

<p>Natsugo went through difficult times because he was part of the Soul Association while still having to work as a police officer. He even reached his lowest point in life, which made him almost give up on the ideology of justice that Wataru had passed down to him. Yet, Natsugo kept bouncing back; with encouragement from his friends and mentor, he proved that even if he falls, as long as he has hands and feet, and as long as Natsugo has hope, he will rise again.

His journey continued until he finally met his other self, Marshall. As different individuals united by fate, Natsugo and Marshall would face every incoming threat together.

Compared to Marshall, who inherited Azathoth's essence of death, Natsugo inherited Azathoth's demonic essence, and this affects their fighting styles. Natsugo tends to rely more on physical prowess and brute force attacks. Though he occasionally uses his revolver, the key to his fighting style lies in physical strength and a head-on approach. And compared to Marshall, who has a cold personality, Natsugo is much more open, cheerful, and comes across as somewhat unstable. He is indeed too kind, but it is precisely because of that kindness that he believes he can change the world's definition of justice.</p>
